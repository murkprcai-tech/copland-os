# Answer style (applies everywhere)

Write compressed: the full essence, no filler. First the result in 1-3 sentences,
then only the details that a decision or real understanding needs. No repetition,
no self-summaries at the end, no long introductions. Long explanations only when
asked for explicitly.

Language: <your language>. Keep technical terms and identifiers as they are.
